PROGRESSION COMPATIBILITY AUDIT — 1.3.0
2026-10-16 Pacific/Guam

Inspected the local active-mod configuration and matched metadata for 1,550 of
1,556 active entries. Core/DLC and entries without local metadata account for the
unmatched entries. ModsConfig reports RimWorld 1.7.4980 rev60. Build and regression
fixtures use the currently installed binaries, rather than relying on that label.

Targeted installed modules: Progression Core, Storage, Kitchen, Genetics,
Production, Agriculture, Temperature and Arsenal; Adaptive Storage Framework;
Medieval Overhaul; Milk Pipe Network; Milk Based Drugs; RJW Unleashed Gastronomy;
RJW Genes, Lactation Expansion and the local RJW Stack Compatibility patch.

Changes:
- Collection bucket follows VFET_Construction when Progression already assigns
  that research to PitLatrine. Sealed storage follows Ferny_Barrels when present.
- SEX_BreastMilk is recognized by the installed Medieval Overhaul, milk-drug and
  RJW Gastronomy milk categories, with no duplicate category entries.
- A separate breast-milk pipe network clones the installed Milk Pipe Network's
  own pipe/tank/drain/tap templates and routes the original breast-milk item both
  into and out of storage. Animal milk does not acquire human-milk identity.
- New pipe endpoints do not register to the animal-milk nutrient-paste grinder.
- Sealing follows a definition marker and ThingOwner storage, so framework class
  replacements or held contents do not silently lose the cleanliness protection.
- Rough and polished fecal walls use normal smoothing links and remain 20 HP.
  They are constructed walls, not naturally generated mineral deposits. They
  do not introduce stone chunks, mining yields, or another world-gen stone type.

Checked without changing behavior:
- Progression Genetics uses native gene definitions and research/quest changes.
  These genes retain standard availability, extraction and active-gene checks.
- Progression Production/Arsenal do not require a new weapon/research tree for
  the manual feces throw. Existing processing recipes retain their normal benches.
- Progression Agriculture/DBH receive their normal biosolids and fertilizer calls.
- Progression Temperature receives nonflammable, airtight walls; no global
  temperature, oxygen or ventilation values are patched.
- Gene-driven production continues to use the single Lactation Expansion reservoir;
  RJW Genes and Coffee do not add another production loop through this integration.

Limits:
Automated native-API/XML/policy/Scribe tests are not a full colony simulation with
all 1,556 mods loaded. Real hauling, research menus, processor recipes, powered pipe
transport and smoothing on a map still need a live-game smoke test. Processor mods
that discard ingredient provenance may not allow Coffee to identify human milk in
finished products; no blanket human-milk tag is applied to animal-milk cheeses.
No dependency files or existing colony saves are edited.
